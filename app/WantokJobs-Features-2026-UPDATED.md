## 3. 🛡️ Security & Trust Features ⭐ **NEW - March 2026**

### Protecting Employers & Jobseekers with AI-Powered Verification

**Launched March 2026** — WantokJobs now includes comprehensive fraud detection and verification systems to ensure platform integrity.

### 🤖 Automated Fraud Detection System

Our AI-powered fraud detector continuously monitors the platform for suspicious activity with **4 detection categories**:

#### 1️⃣ Duplicate Account Detection

- ✅ Identifies employers registering multiple accounts with same email domain
- ✅ Flags similar company names (>70% similarity score)
- ✅ Detects same phone number used across different companies
- ✅ Prevents duplicate account abuse and platform manipulation

**Example Alert:** *"Warning: 3 employer accounts registered from @example.com domain with similar company names (ABC Ltd, ABC PNG, ABC Company)"*

#### 2️⃣ Fake Employer Detection

- ✅ Identifies free email providers (gmail, yahoo, hotmail, outlook) for company accounts
- ✅ Flags generic company names ('Various Employers', NULL, <3 characters)
- ✅ Detects scam keywords in company names ("Investment Opportunity", "Work From Home")
- ✅ Verifies company legitimacy through multiple automated checks

**Example Alert:** *"Suspicious: Company registered with Gmail address and no website verification"*

#### 3️⃣ Mass-Apply Bot Detection

- 🚨 **Critical:** Flags >50 applications in 1 hour from single account
- ⚠️ **High:** Monitors >100 applications in 24 hours
- ✅ Detects identical cover letters across >20 jobs
- ✅ Prevents automated spam applications that waste employer time

**Protection:** Automatic account suspension for severe bot activity

#### 4️⃣ Scam Job Posting Detection

- ✅ Identifies suspicious job descriptions combining scam keywords with unrealistic high salaries (>K10,000)
- ✅ Detects external payment link requests ("Send money to...", "Pay application fee...")
- ✅ Flags suspicious email domains and typosquatting attempts
- ✅ Protects jobseekers from fraudulent job opportunities

**Example Alert:** *"Job flagged: 'Work From Home - Earn K15,000/month' with external payment link"*

---

### ✅ Job & Employer Verification System

Every new job posting and employer account undergoes **automated verification checks**:

#### Verification Checks Include:

**📧 Email Domain Validation**
- MX record lookup to verify email domain exists
- Deliverability testing to ensure emails can be received
- Free email provider detection (gmail, yahoo, hotmail flagged)

**🌐 Website Health Checks**
- Automated HTTP requests to verify company websites are reachable
- SSL certificate validation for security
- Website content analysis for legitimacy signals

**🏛️ PNG IPA Registry Integration**
- Cross-checks company names against official PNG Investment Promotion Authority registry
- Verifies registered business status for PNG companies
- Flags unregistered or mismatched company names

**🔍 Company Information Verification**
- Validates company size, industry classification, and contact details
- Cross-references against historical data and known employers
- Detects inconsistencies in company information

---

### 📊 Confidence Scoring System

Each employer receives a **confidence score (0-100)** based on verification results:

| Score Range | Status | Badge | Meaning |
|-------------|--------|-------|----------|
| **80-100** | ✅ Verified | 🟢 Trusted Employer | Passed all verification checks |
| **50-79** | ⚠️ Partial | 🟡 Review Needed | Some checks failed, manual review recommended |
| **0-49** | 🚨 Flagged | 🔴 Suspicious | Multiple fraud indicators detected |

**High-confidence employers** (80-100) receive:
- ✅ Verified Employer badge on their profile
- 🌟 Priority placement in search results
- 📈 Higher application rates from jobseekers
